About
small and mighty... yeah, that's about how this utility looks.

this bad boy can do so many algos, and you will likely use it for only one or two of them (i bet one of those is gonna be sha256...), and it's written entirely in golang, just how i love it.
and it even has a benchmark mode! who doesn't want one?
Supported Algos
adler32, blake2b-256, blake2b-384, blake2b-512, blake2s-256, blake3, crc32, crc64, fletcher32, fnv-32, fnv-64a, haval-128, haval-160, haval-192, haval-224, haval-256, keccak-256, keccak-512, md2, md4, md5, murmur3-32, ripemd128, ripemd160, ripemd256, ripemd320, sha1, sha224, sha256, sha3-224, sha3-256, sha3-384, sha3-512, sha384, sha512, sha512-256, shake128, shake256, sm3, streebog-256, streebog-512, tiger, tiger2, whirlpool and xxh64!
How To Use
it's actually very simple. just give it a file and it returns its sha256 hash by default! other than that, the help shows pretty much everything it does, simple and easy.
